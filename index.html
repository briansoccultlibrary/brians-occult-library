The Purpose

Knowledge Before Assumption

“The beginning of wisdom is the definition of terms.”

This library is a place for independent study, careful reading, and the comparison of ideas across cultures and centuries. Historical evidence, traditional teachings, and modern interpretations deserve to be examined on their own terms.

Esoteric ideas can inspire profound questions. They should also invite curiosity, context, and critical thought.
